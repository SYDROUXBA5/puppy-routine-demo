Puppy Routine

A calm app for puppy toilet training. You log in one tap, it tells you when to offer the next break and why, and everyone at home shares the same log.

Try the demo

Runs in your browser. No sign-up, nothing to install.

Three things to try

  1. Tap “Pee”It saves straight away, and the next suggested break moves. Tap Undo if you didn't mean it.
  2. Tap “Why”Every suggestion explains itself. It never promises your puppy is safe to wait.
  3. Open ProgressA seven-day view that doesn't count a missed day as a good day.

Inside the app

Best on a phone. Scan this with your phone camera to open the demo. On iPhone you can tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”, to keep it one tap away.

Good to know

  • The puppy, Maple, and her log are made up, so you can play without setting anything up.
  • What you log stays in this browser tab. Reloading starts fresh.
  • This is a preview of an iPhone and Android app that's still being built. Sharing with your household, backups and reminders are only in the phone version.
  • It's a training aid, not veterinary advice. If something seems off with your puppy's toileting, check with your vet.
